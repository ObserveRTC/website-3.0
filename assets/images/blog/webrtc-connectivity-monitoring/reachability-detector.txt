Empty gathering: gathering complete, zero local candidates, never connected.
Timeout/failure: 6,000 ms of stats time without candidates OR disconnected/failed.
A candidate appearing does not prove connection success. It ends the empty-candidate
issue; monitoring continues until connected or closed.
